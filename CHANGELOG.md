## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Trainer for two trucks and two turntable drawbar trailers (four combinations) with realistic coupling behavior and collisions ([#13](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/13), [#6](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/6), [#8](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/8)).
- The app with its menu and driving screen ([#18](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/18)).
- A yard with a street and five exercises, plus a free yard mode ([#11](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/11), [#16](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/16)).
- Four views (cockpit, two mirrors, top-down) that lower their resolution on weak devices, and a display with angle gauges ([#12](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/12), [#14](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/14)).
- Touch, keyboard and mouse controls, including a speed lever that ramps up on W/S and Up/Down ([#9](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/9), [#85](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/85)).
- Scoring with per-criterion results, bests with and without aids, a result screen, and a top-down replay with a timeline, event markers and speed control ([#7](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/7), [#20](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/20), [#84](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/84)).
- German and English UI ([#1](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/1)).
- Installable offline app with an update prompt, update check, a screen that stays on while driving, an offline status and a notice when the device cannot show the graphics ([#5](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/5), [#73](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/73), [#124](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/124)).
- Pause sheet with an unscored restart when leaving a run ([#75](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/75)).
- Top-down aids: zoom and pan, the predicted path for truck and trailer, a marked slalom turn point, and a contact marker naming the body part ([#50](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/50), [#37](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/37), [#74](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/74), [#92](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/92)).
- Clearer exercise goals while driving ([#82](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/82)).
- More realistic cockpit and mirror views: vehicle detail, a sky gradient, contact shadows, haze with an industrial skyline, road markings per the German RMS guidelines, a German industrial yard and the remaining yard props ([#15](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/15), [#49](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/49), [#77](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/77), [#80](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/80), [#81](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/81), [#120](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/120), [#83](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/83), [#91](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/91), [#125](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/125)).
- Loading is retried after a failure, borders are easier to see and the speed lever has a rail ([#86](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/86)).
- Graphics quality presets Low, Balanced and High in settings, defaulting to Balanced ([#134](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/134)).

### Changed

- Smoother edges in the mirrors ([#71](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/71)).
- Content keeps clear of notches and screen edges, mirrors are bigger and narrow windows lay out correctly; on shorter screens the mirrors stay above the controls ([#128](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/128), [#47](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/47)).
- The desktop key legend is collapsed and the top view panels are slimmer ([#102](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/102)).

### Fixed

- Holding W/Up or S/Down ramps the speed lever ([#94](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/94)).
- Cockpit, mirrors and top-down are clearly separated, and grounds, obstacles and halls are distinguishable ([#34](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/34), [#31](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/31)).
- The top-down view draws wheels, axles, turntable and drawbar, keeps a dark outline under aid lines, and frames the whole yard and the follow view clear of the display panels ([#35](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/35), [#76](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/76), [#114](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/114), [#78](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/78), [#106](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/106)).
- The steering wheel shows its position and the straight state, and the controls layout is corrected ([#32](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/32)).
- The forward view from the cockpit is no longer blocked by opaque cab glass, and the skyline draws a quay crane ([#93](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/93), [#117](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/117)).
- The slalom requires weaving between the obstacles, and exercise order and result details are corrected ([#113](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/113), [#103](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/103)).
- The run clock and the with-aids mark start with the first driving input, and the last moment counts when a run ends in the target ([#96](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/96), [#118](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/118)).
- Result screen comparison to the best, keyboard use and leaving a run unscored are corrected; the keyboard keeps working after the pause sheet closes ([#95](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/95), [#101](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/101)).
- The clock rounds correctly and a double click no longer selects text; the cockpit layout fits 1280-pixel-wide windows, and Stop no longer covers the counters at 1024x700 ([#112](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/112), [#107](https://github.com/DocGerd/gegenlenker/pull/107)).
